Eco Ninja Slicing Guides

🎮 Basic Rules

  • Webcam loads automatically; grant permissions when prompted.
  • Hover your index finger tip in front of the camera.
  • Slash plastic bottles, wrappers, batteries, and paper.
  • Do not cut the Earth (🌍) under any circumstances!
  • ⏱️ Timer Mode: You have 3 minutes to slice items and score as much as possible. The game ends only when the timer reaches 00:00, except if you cut the Earth/globe.

💡 Slicing Guides

  • Index finger extended: Activate the cutting blade.
  • Fast movement: Create cut slashes (>16px/frame).
  • Cut Earth (🌍): Immediately exits back to start screen!
⌨️ Keyboard & Mouse Controls: Move mouse across canvas to slash items instantly. Press [Space] to Pause/Resume, [R] to Restart, and [Esc] to exit to the Start Screen.
