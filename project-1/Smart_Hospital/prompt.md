# Digital Hammer Gesture Control Website - Full Page Instructions

## Project Title
Digital Hammer AI Hand Gesture Controlled Website

---

## Design Strategy
To ensure a premium, decluttered, and state-of-the-art user experience, all pages are kept simple and free from repetitive gesture instruction cards. Instead, the **central sidebar HUD (Gesture Directory)** serves as the single source of truth, dynamically highlighting active postures, while each individual page has a clean subtitle text explanation detailing its local features.
