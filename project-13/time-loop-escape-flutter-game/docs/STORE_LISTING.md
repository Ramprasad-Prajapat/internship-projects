## 2. Full Description

**11:57 PM. The clock is ticking. You have five minutes to escape the Grand View Hotel.**

*11:57 — The Last Check-In* is an atmospheric point-and-click time loop mystery adventure game. Trapped in a collapsing temporal loop, you must explore the hotel, discover hidden clues, solve intricate puzzle mechanisms, and interact with mysterious guests and staff before midnight resets the world.

### Features

- ⏳ **The Five-Minute Loop**: Every 300 seconds, time resets to 11:57 PM. The hotel restores itself, but **your knowledge remains intact**.
- 🗝️ **Timeline Anchoring**: Carry a crucial physical item across midnight resets by anchoring it to your timeline.
- 📋 **Persistent Knowledge Board**: Collect clues, secret codes, and dialogue topics that survive every loop reset.
- 🧩 **Intricate Puzzles**: Solve safes, decipher cryptic documents, and crack mechanical locks using gathered evidence.
- 👤 **Dynamic NPC Schedules**: Hotel guests and staff move according to a strict timeline. Be in the right place at the right time.
- 💡 **Progressive Hint System**: Never get permanently stuck. Receive progressive direction without spoiling key revelations.
- 🎬 **Multiple Endings**: Uncover the truth behind the temporal anomaly and unlock multiple narrative endings.
- 📴 **100% Offline & Private**: No internet connection, account creation, or cloud servers required. All save progress stays on your device.
- ⚙️ **Accessibility First**: Customizable audio channels, haptic feedback options, subtitles, reduced motion settings, and large touch targets.
